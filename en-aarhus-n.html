When does it make sense to rent a car in Aarhus N

In the northern part of Aarhus, the need for a rental car often arises where public transport doesn’t reach and your own car isn’t available. It might be a garden project that needs several trips to DIY stores outside the city centre. It might be a trip to IKEA where you need more space than the back seat offers. Or it might be a working day with meetings in Randers, where you’d rather drive yourself than wait for a train. Prices at Oscar are set up so you know exactly what you pay. All rental prices include up to 100 kilometres per day – enough for most trips in and around Aarhus. A trip to Silkeborg and back, or a day of errands in Horsens, usually fits within that limit at no extra cost. If you’re thinking about renting because your car is in for service or repair, it’s an obvious solution. Instead of arranging lifts or fitting in with other people’s schedules, a rental car lets you keep your daily routine without interruption.

Pick-up and return – what to expect

When you arrive at the location on Randersvej, the staff will take you through the car before you drive off. You get a short walk-through of the vehicle’s condition, and any existing damage is recorded so there’s no doubt when you return it. It’s a simple process that needs no previous experience with car rental. The return works the same way – you bring the car back to the location, and the staff confirm they’ve received it. You don’t need to document the car’s condition yourself; it’s done together. If you have questions during the rental period, you’re always welcome to contact Oscar directly. Remember to bring a valid driving licence. The rental agreement is reviewed on site, and you can ask about terms and cover before you sign. What’s agreed at pick-up is what applies – no hidden steps in the process.

Vehicles available at Aarhus N – Randersvej

The location has several categories of vehicles, so you can choose the one that fits your situation. Below is an overview of the three types we offer and what sets them apart.

Cars

If you need flexible transport without extra load space, a car is the obvious choice. The range goes from compact city cars that are easy to park in older neighbourhoods to larger models with plenty of room for passengers and luggage on longer trips. Automatic gearboxes are available in several models – an advantage if you drive a lot in heavy city traffic. Cars can be rented from DKK 329 per day.

Vans

A van gives you load space without needing experience with large vehicles. Vans are easy to manoeuvre in the city and can be parked in most places a car can. The load area is locked and protected, so your things arrive in the same condition they left – whether it’s business goods, white goods or a big shopping trip. Prices start from DKK 389 per day.

Moving trucks

If you’re moving a whole household, a moving truck is the most efficient solution. The large load space cuts the number of trips significantly – and saves you the coordination that comes with driving back and forth. With up to 100 kilometres included per day, the rental usually covers a full move within the Aarhus area at no extra kilometre cost. Prices start from DKK 1,099 per day.

Local conditions that affect your choice of vehicle

Aarhus N is a growing area, and that shows in the traffic. Randersvej and the surrounding roads can be busy at rush hour, especially towards the city centre. If you choose a compact car or a smaller van, you’ll feel the benefit in heavy city traffic and when parking in the older neighbourhoods, where space can be tight. If you’re heading out of town – towards Djursland, Randers or Viborg – it’s worth considering whether you need more comfort or load space for a longer trip. Here the choice of vehicle matters more than for short city trips, and a car with an automatic gearbox can be an advantage if you don’t drive often. With 3,000 vehicles available at Oscar, there’s a good chance you’ll find the right match – even at short notice. The location on Randersvej serves a wide area, and the range reflects the varied needs locally.

Prices for the insurance packages may vary from location to location.

  • You need to bring two documents when you pick up the car: a valid driving licence and your Danish health insurance card (the yellow card). The driving licence must be valid, and the name must match the booking. If you’re not a Danish citizen, bring a valid driving licence and your passport.
